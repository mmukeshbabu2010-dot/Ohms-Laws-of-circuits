Piezoelectric Energy Harvesting

Kinetic Footstep Energy Generator

Harvest ambient mechanical kinetic energy from footsteps using a Piezoelectric disc tile array. This system regulates mechanical pulses into smoothed DC current, stores excess energy into a 12V rechargeable battery cell, and monitors real-time voltage generation via an **Arduino Nano ATmega328P**.
